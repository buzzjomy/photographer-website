About

Hi, we're Piper and Muse

A studio for people who don't love being photographed

We picked up a camera over a decade ago at a friend's wedding, and we've been chasing that same feeling ever since — the quiet, unguarded moment between the posed shots. Today we photograph weddings and portraits full-time, working with couples and individuals who want images that feel true to who they are.

Our approach is relaxed and observational. Minimal posing, lots of walking and talking, and a genuine interest in your story. Whether it's a hundred-person wedding or a solo portrait session, our goal is the same: to make you forget the camera is there.
