## Why these images aren't the final answer

None of these photographers shot for "Piper and Muse" — they're independent Kerala photographers who've shared their work publicly on Wikimedia Commons. They make the site *look* right while you gather the real studio's portfolio, but a real business should launch with the actual photographer's own images, not stock/Commons photos of other people's weddings.
